We do not rush. Every organic grape is hand-harvested at the exact peak of autumn maturity, then cold-pressed within hours to preserve its rich cellular structure.

Our slow cold-brewing process takes twelve full months, allowing the flavor notes to marry naturally in cool stone cellars without chemical preservatives or synthetic additives.
